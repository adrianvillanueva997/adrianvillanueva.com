# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The site is a personal space that serves a mix of technical readers, potential collaborators or employers, and people interested in Adrian's work and interests. No single professional audience should displace its role as a personal home.

## Product Purpose

Share and document Adrian's writing, projects, and personal interests over time, while making it easy to start relevant conversations about opportunities and collaboration.

## Positioning

A personal website belonging to Adrian Villanueva Martinez, bringing his work in software engineering, data infrastructure, systems, and developer tools together with his interests outside work.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the site's personal identity and the breadth of its professional and personal content.
- Keep claims accurate and the voice authentic; do not invent accomplishments or evidence.
- Visitors can explore work, writing, a résumé, personal updates, music, and tools, and contact Adrian.

## Evidence on Hand

- Project descriptions: `src/data/projects.yaml` and `src/data/selected-work.yaml`.
- Professional history: `src/data/resume.yaml`.
- Writing data: `src/data/metalops.json`.
- Personal content: `src/content/` and `src/data/music.yaml`.

## Product Principles

- Treat the site as a personal home, not solely a professional pitch.
- Give professional work and personal interests room to coexist.
- Make relevant conversations easy to start.
- Ground claims in real work and preserve Adrian's identity and voice.
