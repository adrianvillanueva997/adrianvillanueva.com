## HOW I WORK

I like systems that are resilient, understandable, and a little boring in
production. My approach is simple:

- **Simplicity over complexity** - Use the right tool for the job

- **Developer experience** - Make platforms pleasant to use

- **Understanding before implementation** - Find the real problem first

- **Leave a signal behind** - Document the decisions that matter
